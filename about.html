About Us

Welcome to Royal African College, a premier institution for higher education in Africa, offering 100% online, flexible, and affordable learning for students across the continent.

Our mission is to provide accessible, high-quality education to empower students to achieve their academic and professional dreams without financial barriers.

Our Mission

To democratize higher education across Africa by providing tuition-free, globally recognized degrees that empower students to transform their lives and communities.

Our Vision

To become Africa's leading online educational institution, breaking down barriers to quality education and creating pathways to prosperity for millions of students.

We Are Global

With bases in Malawi, Kenya, and the United Kingdom, we connect African students with world-class education, technology, and opportunities.
